    G / READ THIS FIRST

    Honest caveats

    ✳
    • The test sets are small. Each result shows how many images it is based on (n). With 10 images, one mistake moves accuracy by 10 points, so small differences between versions may be noise.
    • Confidence is uncalibrated. A 90% score does not mean the model is right 90% of the time. ECE shows how far confidence and accuracy disagree.
    • The flight schedule plays no part here. These numbers score the airline classifier only; the schedule lookup in the Identify tab uses synthetic demo data.
    • “Selected by validation” marks the new (v2) version with the best accuracy on the 13 validation images; ties are broken by lower log loss, which can favour a model trained without label smoothing. The test sets were not used to choose it.
